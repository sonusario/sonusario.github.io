Presets

Ember is Conway plus depletion: crowded cells burn out, sparse ones recover.

Clamp 0–100%

Limits every birth, death and depletion rate, including the master sliders.

Birth neighbourhood 8 cells

Which offsets a dead cell scans for live neighbours. Centre is the cell itself.

Birth chance per live neighbour

Death neighbourhood 8 cells

Which offsets a live cell scans for live neighbours.

Death chance per live neighbour

Depletion threshold off

Every cell carries a reserve counter. At 0 the whole depletion pass is skipped. Above 0, any cell whose counter reaches the threshold is forced dead and stays dead until the counter falls back.

Depletion neighbourhood 8 cells

Which offsets feed the counter roll. Live and dead cells both roll.

Counter step per live neighbour

One roll per generation. A hit moves the counter by one — up by default, down when − is ticked. 0% never fires, 100% always fires.

Grid 70 × 50

Resizing the grid resamples what is already drawn.

Colour source

Live cells shade by their death chance, dead cells by their birth chance.
